# Netoraria agent charter
Netoraria is the canonical name; H·ARCHIVE is historical lineage only. This repo is the source of truth after bootstrap. All work must foreground visual design, clear information architecture, mobile-first responsiveness, accessibility, cover-forward discovery, original works and episode pages, character profiles, licensed visual provenance, source materials (game/manga/doujinshi), studios, timelines, graph relationships and honest gaps. A wiki useful to animation fans comes FIRST; scholarly history and education complement rather than replace catalog discovery. Do not claim generic hentai site claims have been verified; cite primary Japanese publisher/product records where possible, use third-party databases for leads and attribution. Do not bulk scrape without permission. Every work/episode/edition/character/relationship has separate identity. Distinguish adaptations from mere fan speculation. Only show covers/assets when rights or license is known; otherwise use attractive authored cover tiles. Non-graphic, adult-oriented metadata only. Historical discussion of potentially underage characters is contextual and neutral, no sexualized minor imagery, descriptions, links or recommendations. Avoid unreviewed public deployments and user data collection. Work in review branches and PRs. Visual UX is a blocking product requirement, not optional polish.

## Permanent product strategy: completion-first, honest confidence

The fan wiki should make the genre broadly discoverable before exhaustive criticism or individual artist biographies, while clearly distinguishing **unverified discovery candidates** from **canonical verified works**. The source of truth is `data/catalog.json` with `discovery_candidates`, `taxonomy`, `coverage_objectives` and older verified work/release tables. On each research sprint, search genre-specific anime indices and publisher catalogs for *more unique candidate titles*, normalize Japanese original name, romaji, aliases, year, dates, source and tentative tag basis; do not inflate completeness by reissues, renamed series, or duplicated episodes. Cross-check age/consent/rights; context-only or school-age/age-uncertain works must not enter public promotional discovery. Tags Netorare, Netori, Netorase, cheating, infidelity, BSS, jealousy and consensual nonmonogamy have **distinct semantic axes**. Source-specific tag membership requires actual access to the category entry; do not claim completeness when a source page is inaccessible. Never bulk scrape without authorization, embed pirate playback or copy unlicensed images. Every public provisional candidate needs clear unverified status and no unfounded personal/sexual imagery. A real global completion percentage requires a sourced denominator. Preserve strong mobile visual UX and sources. See `docs/COMPLETION_FIRST_v08.md`.
