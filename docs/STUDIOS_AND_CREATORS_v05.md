# Netoraria v0.5 · Studio & Artist Research Network
Date: 2026-10-13 | Status: attribution-based research sample, not an exhaustive industry history.

## Taxonomy: don't flatten a production chain

- **Animation studio** means the credited animation production contractor, Japanese `制作` (e.g. T-REX).
- **Release label** means the catalog/imprint used to market and distribute the OVA (e.g. あんてきぬすっ / Antekinussu, Queen Bee).
- **Publisher / catalog owner** means the portal/business maintaining product listings (e.g. Rune/Lune, Media Bank). A linked publisher does **not** automatically establish the actual animation studio.
- **Original game brand** means the game developer/publisher credited as source (Rune TeamBitters, ORC SOFT).
- **Original comic circle and manga publisher** mean where a source work originated (V-Pan's Ecstasy, DOLL PLAY, Wanimagazine).
- **Artist/creator** is a distinct person or credited pseudonym with a documented role (original artist, director, screenplay, storyboard, character concept). Do not imply the same staff contributed to every installment without credit documentation.

## Newly traced artist paths

- **Satou Kuuki (左藤空気):** Kegareboshi and the earlier Netokano comic adaptation. https://www.lune-soft.jp/ova/30803 and https://www.lune-soft.jp/ova/21475 . The earlier work is not publicly promoted due age issues.
- **Kurozu Gatari (黒巣ガタリ):** Natsuzuma original art; the animation staff are credited separately. https://www.lune-soft.jp/ova/29798
- **Minamoto (みな本):** the marriage-themed original comic adapted as the 2023 two-part Tsuma ni Damatte Sokubaikai ni Iku n Janakatta. This work is confirmed as an Antekinussu release. Animation-studio attribution is a **third-party lead**, not accepted as a primary-confirmed credit pending product-page corroboration. https://www.lune-soft.jp/news/26424
- **Raikaken (雷火剣):** credited as director on Kegareboshi, Natsuzuma and the two earlier game-derived OVAs in this sample; credits visible on individual publisher pages.
- **Tokku03 (特区03):** screenplay credited on Kegareboshi and Natsuzuma.
- **Sagari Megane (サガリ眼鏡):** storyboard credit across multiple T-REX OVAs.
- **Tsujimura Umeko, Tatsumi, Gentle Sasaki and Suruga Kreutz:** screenplay or character concept for the two game-derived works (source pages above).
- **Aijou Riku:** commercial manga original artist for Yume Miru Otome, not automatically a doujin creator; contextual archival-only due publisher characterization. https://www.lune-soft.jp/ova/29228

## Queen Bee / Media Bank

Media Bank's official Queen Bee release-label catalog lists the March 6, 2026 *Sakuramiya Shimai no Netorare Kiroku* **complete edition**, credited to 史鬼匠人 and product QNB-M178. This is proof of a 2026 **edition**, not the release of a new animated episode or production by a studio named Queen Bee. Source: https://www.mediabank.co.jp/label.php?id=22&pg=1&stat=0 .

We do **not** presently have a manufacturer-verified original-episode split or age assurance for that work. It remains a research lead and the Queen Bee / Media Bank organizational relationship is recorded separately, not as an invented T-REX-style production credit.
